The topic of the CSI group during their fellowships has been "Representations in the cognitives sciences: Theoretical linguistics, psycholinguistics, cognitive psychology, experimental psychology, theoretical philosophy, computer science". 

In the kick-off retreat in December 2025 in the wonderful location in Trifels we discussed how we could tackle the topic from our different perspectives and disciplines. It became clear quickly that our respective disclipines use the term "representation" quite differently: for example, as relational or non-relational concept with reference to the external world (or not). We decided to work on a taxonomy of representations that would then be the basis for a joint paper. The blog entries document our regular meetings throughout the fellowship (semester) to make progress in this endeavour.

In the wrap-up retreat in June 2026, again in wonderful Trifels, we got together to bring together our different ideas that we had summarized in blurbs for our respective disciplines. Despite the massive heatwave that hit Germany at exactly this time, and thanks to the AC in the beautiful seminar room in Trifels, we managed to combine our different sections and write up a draft for a paper on the topic of representations.
