This week, we finally came back to our discussion on representations. We had a lively debate on recurring questions that we identified in our different approaches to representation:

- What are (mental) representations?
- How are representations structured?
- What is the content of representations?
- Are representations observable or latent?
- What are the function(s) of representations?
- How are representations grounded?
- What is the relation of representations to the external world?
- Is research in your field possible without reference to representations?
 
Based on these recurrent questions, we tried to sketch a model that identifies different levels of representation. We aim to further flesh this out to provide a taxonomy that allows us to capture different aspects and/or theoretical assumptions that we and our respective fields make. We also plan to discuss these questions in more details with our guests that we will welcome in the weeks after the Easter break.
