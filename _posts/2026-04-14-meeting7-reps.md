This week's meeting was the first meeting after our two-week Easter break. On Monday we listened to the super interesting talk of our colleague Colin Phillips that we invited to our CSI lecture series. The topic was "Specific and general mechanisms in real-time linguistic computation" which also tackled the concept of (linguistic) representations. We continued our discussion on the topic on Tuesday and got the impression that Colin gave us a lot of food for thought for our enterprise.
