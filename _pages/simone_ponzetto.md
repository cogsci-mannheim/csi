I lead the Natural Language Processing (NLP) group and have been working at the fascinating interface of language and computation for most of my academic life. My research focuses on natural language processing, knowledge acquisition, and AI, with a strong interest in the interfaces between NLP and other disciplines. Within the CAS initiative, I am particularly interested in how humans and large language models align — and differ — in terms of performance, processes, and representations. This is especially relevant in an exciting age like ours, where artificial models seem to be on a path toward achieving and even surpassing human-level performance, something that would have been difficult to predict only a few years ago!
