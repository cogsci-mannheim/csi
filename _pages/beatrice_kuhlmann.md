I am a cognitive psychologist researching (meta-)memory and aging-related changes therein with behavioral/experimental paradigms and cognitive modeling. I am particularly interested in processes modifying memory, such as encoding strategies, as well as compensation of memory deficits, such as through educated guessing. At CAS, I am intrigued by the challenge of more precisely defining the processes and capacities I am interested in to be able to better communicate about them with scholars from other disciplines and I am interested to dive deeper into analogies between the human mind and artificial intelligence.
